# Privacy Policy - `ipo-india-hub` (`r/IPO_India`)

1. **Zero Personal Financial Identifiers (Zero PII):**
   `ipo-india-hub` never requests, collects, processes, or stores personally identifiable financial information, including Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), IPO Application Numbers, 16-digit Demat BOIDs, bank account numbers, UPI IDs, email addresses, or phone numbers.
2. **Anonymous Reddit `userId` Keying & 30-Day Sliding Inactivity Permanent Deletion:**
   When a signed-in Redditor voluntarily participates in polls (`Applying` vs `Skipping`, allotment feedback, listing polls), follows a DRHP filing, saves a Personal Watchlist, submits a Listing Price Prediction, or logs entries in `My IPO Scorecard`, `ipo-india-hub` stores your internal Reddit `userId` (`t2_*`) alongside those selections inside Reddit's built-in Devvit Redis storage with an automatic **30-day sliding inactivity TTL** (refreshed whenever you visit the hub or save updates, and enforced via automated daily cleanup across both private keys and shared voter hashes to follow Reddit Devvit data-minimization guidelines). **All data of inactive users is permanently deleted after 30 consecutive days of inactivity and cannot be retrieved or recovered at all.** Open the hub at least once a month to keep your 30-day timer reset, or export/restore local JSON backups of your Scorecard anytime. Public leaderboard rankings display anonymous labels (`Redditor #...`) unless a member explicitly opts in with their Reddit username.
3. **Community Reports, Live Chat & Missing-IPO Submissions:**
   - Voluntary `Report Wrong Data` flags (`ipo:data_reports:v1`), `Submit Missing IPO` requests (`ipo:user_submissions:v1`), and Live Chat message reports (`ipo:chat:reports:v1:*`, 24-hour TTL) store an opaque salted SHA-256 hash (`16` hex characters) solely for duplicate prevention and spam rate-limiting (never storing plain-text reporter identities). Live Chat reports forward directly to the subreddit's native Reddit Mod Queue.
   - **Live Chat (`asUser: SUBMIT_COMMENT`):** Sending a normal message in Live Chat posts a **public Reddit comment** under your Reddit account (`u/username`) on the active weekly Slot 1 Hub post, triggered strictly by your explicit Send action. A pre-submit Indian PII shield blocks PAN numbers, 16-digit Demat BOIDs, 10-digit phone numbers, and emails before posting. Private slash commands (`/ipo`, `/gmp`, `/odds`, `/unblock`, `/registrar`, `/calendar`, `/help`) are returned ephemerally only to you and are never posted to Reddit. A rolling cache of the latest `150` public chat comments is stored in Devvit Redis with a `7`-day TTL and automatically stays synchronized with `onCommentUpdate`, `onCommentDelete`, and `onModAction` removals.
4. **Granular Controls, 1-Click Full Deletion & Account Deletion Triggers:**
   You can clear individual poll votes (`Clear Vote`), clear price predictions (`Clear Guess`), delete individual Scorecard rows, or permanently erase 100% of stored user-keyed data (Scorecard, Watchlist, Leaderboard opt-in, votes, predictions, DRHP follows, reports, missing-IPO submissions, and cached Live Chat copies) via **Delete All My App Data** under **FAQ -> Terms, Privacy & Delete My Data**. Note that public Reddit comments posted via Live Chat stay on Reddit natively (open any comment on Reddit to edit or delete it there, and `onCommentUpdate` / `onCommentDelete` syncs the change automatically).
5. **Zero External Transmission of Reddit User Data:**
   Zero Reddit user IDs, usernames, votes, predictions, or scorecard entries are ever sent outside Reddit. External `fetch()` calls are used exclusively server-side to read public market data feeds (`fypqhnwdczmsgwmvxidc.supabase.co`) and when moderators parse public document screenshots (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`).
6. **Privacy Contact:**
   For privacy requests or questions, contact subreddit moderators via **r/IPO_India Modmail**.
